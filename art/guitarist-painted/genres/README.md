# Painted genre guitarists

Twenty-four finished pictures. One man and one woman for each of twelve genres: rock, punk, metal, blues, country, folk, jazz, classical, indie, funk, reggae and pop. Each file is one complete painting. These are not layers, and they are not stacked.

They follow the painted pilot. Soft dimensional cartoon, warm light from the upper left, head to about the middle of the thigh, holding a guitar, turned a little toward the left. Classical is seated. The backdrop is transparent. The edge was checked on the dark ground (#181614) and the light ground (#F0EADE).

Files are named `<genre>-m.webp` and `<genre>-w.webp`. The canvas is 800 by 800.

These pictures are not wired into the app. `pilot/`, `pilot2/`, `pilot3/` and `pilot4/` are unchanged.
