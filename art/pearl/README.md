# Pearl icons

White mother-of-pearl pieces painted to match the polished pearl pick inlay, art/road-neck/inlays/pick.webp: smooth and glossy, soft milky clouds, light from the top left, a gentle soft edge, no colour tint, transparent background. The white files stay untinted so the app can colour them.

- heart.webp: 360 x 360 px, a full rounded heart, for Favorites and the Ko-fi button.
- coin.webp: 360 x 360 px, a round coin with a raised rim and a dollar sign cut through, for Merch.
- medal.webp: 360 x 360 px, a round medal with two short ribbon tails, for Milestones.
- amp.webp: 360 x 360 px, a small guitar amp from the front with a speaker cut on the left and two knobs on the right, for Your band.
- fork.webp: 360 x 360 px, a tuning fork with two thick prongs on a short handle, for the Tuner.
- share.webp: 360 x 360 px, a thick upward arrow rising out of an open tray, for Share.
- info.webp: 360 x 360 px, a solid disc with a bold lowercase i cut through, for Help.
- bar.webp: 512 x 64 px, a flat pearl strip with square ends that repeats left to right with no seam, for progress bars.

The -teal files are the same shapes dyed as smooth teal mother-of-pearl in the app accent #00C8B8 (--accent / --rr-amber in styles.css), with lighter cyan-white highlights and deeper teal in the darker areas.

- heart-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same heart in the app's teal, for Favorites and the Ko-fi button.
- coin-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same coin in the app's teal, for Merch.
- medal-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same medal in the app's teal, for Milestones.
- amp-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same amp in the app's teal, for Your band.
- fork-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same tuning fork in the app's teal, for the Tuner.
- share-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same share arrow in the app's teal, for Share.
- info-teal.webp: 360 x 360 px, the same info disc in the app's teal, for Help.
- bar-teal.webp: 512 x 64 px, the same progress bar strip in the app's teal, seamless left to right.
